Vertical Scroller - early test build
===================================

Thanks for helping test! The art is placeholder shapes for now; what matters
is how it plays, alone or with friends.

1. Before unzipping: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock"
   (bottom of the General tab) > OK. Then extract it.
   This stops Windows from warning about the game.

   Already extracted, or no Unblock box? Double-click VerticalScroller.exe
   anyway.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then
   "Run anyway". It warns because the game is new and isn't signed by a big
   publisher, not because anything is wrong.

2. Type your name. Play Solo, or click "Join Game", paste the invite your
   host sent you (looks like 203.0.113.5:7777) and click Join.

3. In the lobby, click your ship's name to pick one of 5 ships (hover for
   what its gun does) and your special (Mega Bomb or Shield), then press
   Ready. The host starts the game.

Everyone needs the same build: if the host sends a new zip, use that one.

A run: Planet Base (about 4 minutes, a boss at the end) -> results -> shop
-> Planet Base again, harder and with a twist -> run summary.
The team shares 3 spare ships: if you're shot down you come back after
3 seconds, until they run out.

Controls (keyboard, gamepad, or mouse mode from Settings):
  Move      WASD / arrow keys / left stick
  Focus     hold Shift or Z / RT or A: slower, stronger narrow shot,
            and your tiny hitbox is shown
  Special   Space or X / B or RB (charges refill each stage)
  Menu      Esc / Start (Settings and Leave game are in there)
  The gun fires by itself. Settings can change that (Toggle or Hold, then
  F / C / Y fires) and switch the screen scaling.

Pick up gold Scrap (spent in the shop), blue power orbs (stronger gun),
and rare boosts.

Hosting: click "Host Game"; the lobby shows your invite and copies it to
the clipboard. If friends can't connect, open UDP port 7777 on your router.
