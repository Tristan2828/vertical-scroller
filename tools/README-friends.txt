Vertical Scroller - early test build
===================================

Thanks for helping test! This is a very early build: fly, shoot, and see
how it feels with friends. Nobody can take damage or die yet.

1. Before unzipping: right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock"
   (bottom of the General tab) > OK. Then extract it.
   This stops Windows from warning about the game.

   Already extracted, or no Unblock box? Double-click VerticalScroller.exe
   anyway.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then
   "Run anyway". It warns because the game is new and isn't signed by a big
   publisher, not because anything is wrong.

2. Type your name, click "Join Game", paste the invite your host sent you
   (looks like 203.0.113.5:7777) and click Join.

3. In the lobby, press Ready. The host starts the game.

Everyone needs the same build: if the host sends a new zip, use that one.

Controls (keyboard, gamepad, or mouse mode from Settings):
  Move      WASD / arrow keys / left stick
  Focus     hold Shift or Z / RT or A: slower, stronger narrow shot,
            and your tiny hitbox is shown
  Menu      Esc / Start (Settings and Leave game are in there)
  The gun fires by itself. Settings can change that (Toggle or Hold, then
  F / C / Y fires) and switch the screen scaling.

Can't connect? Tell your host: they may need to open UDP port 7777 on
their router.
