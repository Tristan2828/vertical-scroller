A testing build: everything from v0.0.9 plus sound, music and in-game updates. Everyone in a game needs this same version.

**Download this one by hand** (v0.0.9 and older can't update themselves). From now on, new versions show up on the title screen with an **Update now** button.

**New**
- **Sound and music**: placeholder synth sound effects (shots, hits, explosions, pickups, Mega Bomb, Shield, the boss alarm...) and four music tracks (menu, stage, boss, shop). Volume, Sound effects and Music are in Settings.
- **Screen shake** on big hits and explosions (Settings: Off / Low / Full).
- **Updates in the game**: the title screen shows your version in the bottom-left corner and offers **Update now** when a newer release is out.
- **Send feedback** on the title menu and the Esc menu, with an optional screenshot.
- **The game opens on your widest monitor** (no more thin band on a portrait screen), and remembers the monitor you last played on.

**What to tell us**
Are the sounds too loud, too quiet or annoying (the gun plays all the time)? Does the music fit? Does the screen shake feel good or too much?
