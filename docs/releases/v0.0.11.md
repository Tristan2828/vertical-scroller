A small fix build on top of v0.0.10. Everyone in a game needs this same version: on v0.0.10, use **Update now** on the title screen.

**Fixed**
- Clicking a Settings button no longer takes the game out of fullscreen (or un-maximizes the window).

**New**
- Volume, Effects and Music are sliders now (drag them, or left / right on a gamepad). The Effects slider plays a sound while it moves so you can hear the level.

**Tip**
If your game opens in a window, turn **Fullscreen** on in Settings once; it sticks now. **Scaling: Sharp** always fills the screen; Pixel-perfect can leave black borders.
