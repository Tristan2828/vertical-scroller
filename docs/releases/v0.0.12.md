An art test build: everything from v0.0.11 plus a first look at real pixel art. Everyone in a game needs this same version: on v0.0.10 or v0.0.11, use **Update now** on the title screen.

**New**
- **Mission selector** in the lobby (the host picks): the classic gray-box **Planet Base**, or the same stage drawn with downloaded art: **Kla'ed** (red), **Nairan** (green) and **Nautolan** (brown) enemy fleets, and **Shoot'em Up** enemies. Same waves, same boss: only the look changes. Tanks use new art too; the rest of the ground is still gray boxes.
- **Choose your ship**: click your ship in the lobby to pick a ship, then a **skin** that suits it (Classic or one of the new pixel-art ships), shown in your player color. Skins are looks only.
- The game's art credits are in `CREDITS.md` in the zip.

**What to tell us**
Which enemy fleet looks best? Which skins do you like, and which don't fit? Do the new enemies read clearly against the ground and the bullets?
